# Product Domains

Domain pages are lightweight landing pages for important business concepts.

They should help discovery by linking to canonical vocabulary, durable product rules, relevant architecture, durable decisions, and verification entry points.

Link coherent business explanations at their current authoritative paths; new
ones normally use `docs/product/specs/`. A link is navigation, not confirmation
of an ordinary explanation or candidate Rule.

Do not duplicate the underlying facts. A domain page is a router, not a second wiki.
