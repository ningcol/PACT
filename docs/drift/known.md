# Known Drift Register

This file records known inconsistencies accepted during brownfield adoption or deferred cleanup.

An entry is not permission to ignore risk indefinitely. It prevents agents from repeatedly rediscovering or silently rewriting accepted drift.

## Template

```md
## DRIFT-NNN — Title

Status: known
Severity: low | medium | high

### Conflict

What artifacts/behaviors disagree?

### Current treatment

Which source should agents follow for now?

### Why deferred

Why is this not fixed immediately?

### Revisit when

What task/event should trigger resolution?
```
